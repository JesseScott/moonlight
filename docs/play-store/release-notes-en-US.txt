Moonlight 0.7.3

New in 0.7: a live wallpaper and a home screen widget (see About). The colours follow the moon: hue from the phase, richer when the moon is high, darker below the horizon, using your approximate location (kept on your device). A welcome page explains this before Android asks. Usage data and crash reports are one switch in About, off unless you turn it on. Better with large text, on tablets and with screen readers.
